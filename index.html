Privacy Policy for CaloriScan Effective Date: September 27, 2026 Welcome to CaloriScan. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application. 1. Information We Collect We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us and data that is collected automatically when you use CaloriScan: • Camera and Photo Library Data: To provide our core functionality, the app requires access to your device's camera and photo gallery. We collect the images of food you choose to scan or upload. • Usage Data (Freemium Limits): We track the number of scans you perform daily and monthly to enforce our free-tier limits (4 scans per day, 20 scans per month) and manage premium subscriptions. • Device and Log Data: We automatically collect standard diagnostic data, including your device type, operating system, IP address, and app crash reports, to ensure the app functions properly. • Purchase Data: If you upgrade to our Premium tier, payment processing is handled entirely by your app store provider (Apple App Store or Google Play). We do not collect or store your credit card or direct billing information. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the collected information for the following purposes: • To estimate calories and generate nutritional reports based on the photos you submit. • To enforce scan limits for free-tier users and manage premium access. • To monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve the user experience. • To identify and fix technical bugs or crashes within the app. 3. Third-Party Services and AI Processing CaloriScan relies on third-party services to function. By using the app, you acknowledge that your food photos are transmitted to and processed by external AI providers. • Google Gemini API: We use Google's Gemini AI to analyze your food photos and generate the nutritional reports. The images you scan are sent securely to Google's servers strictly for this analysis. Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for information on how they handle API data. • Payment Processors: Apple or Google process your payment information if you choose to subscribe to the premium tier. 4. Data Retention • Images: Photos uploaded for analysis are processed in real-time. We do not permanently store your food photos on our servers after the nutritional report is generated and returned to your device. • Usage Data: Scan counts and subscription status are retained locally on your device and securely synced with our backend to maintain your account limits. 5. Security of Your Information We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse. 6. Children's Privacy CaloriScan does not knowingly solicit information from or market to children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under age 13 without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information as quickly as possible. 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.
